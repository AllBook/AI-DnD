How to play

  1. Gather. One player creates a party and shares the invite. Up to five can sit at the table. Everyone picks a class; the host picks the adventure and begins.
  2. Say what you do. In a scene, type what your character does or says, or ask the Dungeon Master a question. Everything sent while the DM is busy is answered together in the next reply.
  3. Roll when asked. When the DM calls for a check, the named player presses Roll d20. The dice decide, and everyone sees the result.
  4. Fight in turns. Combat uses buttons: pick a target, then attack, use an ability or spell, defend, or improvise a stunt. You can still ask the DM questions in a fight.
  5. Table talk is chat between players. The DM does not read it.

Commands: /roll 2d6+3 rolls dice for all to see, /recap asks for the story so far, /ooc text sends table talk, /help opens this page.

Keys: Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line, Escape stops the narration.

Leave this party?

Leaving gives up your seat and your character. To take a break and come back to the same character, close the tab instead: your seat is kept.
